Scenic Water Mirage

Taj Lake Palace & Pichola

A breathtaking white marble sanctuary floating majestically upon the azure waters of Lake Pichola. Built as a summer pleasure palace, it represents the absolute zenith of Mewari luxury.

Imperial Mewar Throne

Udaipur City Palace

A towering fortress of granite and crystal rising proudly over the lake, showcasing unparalleled architectural grandeur.

Divine Architecture

Jagdish Temple

An intricately carved ancient Hindu temple located just outside the Royal Palace, dedicated to Lord Vishnu and a marvel of Indo-Aryan architecture.

Lakeside Heritage

Gangaur Ghat

A prominent main ghat on Lake Pichola surrounded by majestic havelis, famously known as the prime location for vibrant cultural festivals.

Peak of Sunsets

Sajjangarh Monsoon Palace

A majestic mountaintop fortress overlooking the entire valley, floating amidst clouds with panoramic sunset views.
